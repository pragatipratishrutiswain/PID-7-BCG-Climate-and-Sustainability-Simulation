## Scope 3 emissions
✨ **Scope 3 emissions** can be complex to calculate and address. But that doesn’t make them any less important for companies who want to significantly reduce their emissions.

- Fast Cars has asked us to look at how they could reduce their upstream scope 3 emissions. 
- But at BCG we know that the downstream scope 3 emissions make up the greatest proportion of emissions from our automotive clients. 
- These emissions are mostly from people using the cars they bought from Fast Cars.

# About Upstream and Downstream Emissions
Upstream Emissions include emissions generated from activities in pre-production.

Downstream Emissions include emissions generated from activities in post-production.
